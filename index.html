I made this website because I noticed that Cooper Black is just EVERYWHERE, and I wanted a place to display all of my finds. I hope someone finds this at least a little bit funny or this enlightens people to how much Cooper Black is out there. To me, Cooper Black is just so distinct and easy to spot once you know what you're looking for. Maybe by exposing people to it, more people will begin to see it everywhere like I do. The image to the left should give you a good idea on what this typeface looks like.

Mostly I just find it funny to point it out when this typeface appears because it annoys my friends. But on a deeper level, it is really cool to see how a typeface thats over 100 years old is still so popular.
